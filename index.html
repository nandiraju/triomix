Triomix

Integrated DNA, RNA, and Protein Study for Bioinformatics

Triomix is a next-generation bioinformatics tool developed and owned by 1Cell.Ai. It delivers comprehensive analysis across genomics, transcriptomics, and proteomics, enabling researchers to study the critical flow of genetic information from DNA (transcription) to RNA (translation) to Proteins.

Accelerating biotechnology research, Triomix harnesses advanced biological computational models to provide structured, multi-dimensional profiles of cellular systems.
